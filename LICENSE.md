# Rights and license

The historical printed works by August Wünsche (d. 1912), including the Mechiltha with Jakob Winter, are distinct from the modern digital preparation. Upstream public-domain and license metadata are preserved in each data file.

- Sefaria's Bereshit and Vayikra Wünsche editions are explicitly marked **Public Domain**.
- Sefaria's Shemot Wünsche edition (Leipzig 1882) has no license field in the export and an empty license value in its version listing. No explicit digital license has been invented for it.
- The ten Safran editions retain their existing metadata, including Public Domain notices where supplied. Two source files (Bamidbar and Devarim) have no explicit license field.
- Newly contributed documentation and editorial preparation in this repository: **CC BY 4.0**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Attribution: Igor Mendel Itkin (ed.), August Wünsche – Midraschim, with the commit or version used.
- Scripts: **MIT**; see [scripts/LICENSE](scripts/LICENSE). Copied Safran scripts retain their upstream copyright notice through that license.
- Portrait: copied unchanged from Safran; its exact source is recorded in the manifest and portraits/README.md. No separate image-license assertion is added.

These terms do not replace third-party source rights or restrict underlying public-domain material. Please retain the edition, digitization and funding acknowledgments in the metadata.
